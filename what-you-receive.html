LAKELAND SURVEYING · Lavallette

What you receive

Every survey is performed under a New Jersey professional land surveyor and delivered as a signed, sealed plan. The seal carries the license number.

  • Boundary: a map of the lot, the corners, and the monuments found or set, plus notes on visible encroachments.
  • Elevation certificate: the FEMA form with the measured floors and grades, for the insurer or the town.
  • Title survey: the map the title company or attorney asked for, with the exceptions from the commitment checked in the field.
  • Stakeout: marks on the ground for the builder, on the date scheduled.
