LAKELAND SURVEYING

Elevation certificate for flood insurance

If an insurer or a town asks for an elevation certificate, they mean the FEMA form, not a full topographic survey. Lakeland measures the floors and the adjacent grade and returns the signed certificate.

In Ocean County a straightforward residential elevation certificate commonly runs about $600 to $1,500. A raised shore house or a hard-to-reach crawl space can cost more. Turnaround is often a few days to two weeks. This is a range, not a quote.
