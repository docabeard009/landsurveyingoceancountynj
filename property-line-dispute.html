LAKELAND SURVEYING

Property line dispute

If you and a neighbor disagree on the line, the document that holds up is a boundary survey signed by a New Jersey professional land surveyor. Lakeland maps the deed, recovers or sets the corners, and notes a fence, a wall, or a driveway that crosses the line.

In Ocean County a straightforward residential boundary survey commonly runs about $800 to $3,500. A dispute, a missing corner, or a barrier-island lot often costs more. That is a range, not a quote. Turnaround is often one to two weeks after the records and the field work.
