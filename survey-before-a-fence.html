Survey before a fence

A fence built on the wrong side of the line is the usual way a neighbor dispute starts. A boundary survey locates the corners before the posts go in. If a corner is missing, the crew sets one.

In Ocean County a straightforward residential boundary survey commonly runs about $800 to $3,500. A barrier-island lot often costs more. Turnaround is often one to two weeks. This is a range, not a quote.
